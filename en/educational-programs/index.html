Become a quality expert: ISTQB® Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL)

Become a quality expert: ISTQB® Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) Quality assurance is the key to the success of any software project. With the ISTQB® Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) you can start your career in software testing or consolidate your position as an expert in quality assurance.

Our comprehensive training optimally prepares you for the official ISTQB® certification and provides you with practical knowledge that can be used immediately in your daily work. This training is aimed at both beginners and experts who want to deepen their knowledge.

What you will learn in the ISTQB® CTFL training

  • The basics of software testing: What is testing, why is it necessary, and which testing principles and processes should you apply? We teach you the essential concepts of software testing.
  • Testing in the software development cycle: You will learn how tests are integrated into different phases of software development and how to work efficiently in the different test stages such as unit, integration or system tests.
  • Static testing and reviews: Learn how to use static testing methods such as inspections or walkthroughs to detect and correct errors at an early stage.
  • Test design techniques: Get an overview of black-box and white-box methods and learn how to develop effective test cases to cover all relevant scenarios.
  • Test management: Plan and monitor test activities, manage risks and execute tests efficiently in different development environments.

Test tools: Find out which tools make your daily test work easier and how automation tools can be used effectively.

The training programme

  • Day 1: Introduction to the basics of testing. You will learn the basics for recognising errors and ensuring the quality of your software projects. We also discuss the life cycle of software projects and how testing is integrated into development.
  • Day 2: Focus on test analysis and design, where you will dive deep into the development of test scenarios. The day is rounded off with an introduction to test management.
  • Day 3: Test tools and advanced techniques to increase the efficiency and automation of test processes.

Why is the ISTQB® certification valuable?

  • Global recognition: The ISTQB® certification is recognised worldwide and is considered a seal of quality for testing professionals. It shows that you have a sound understanding of testing techniques and principles.
  • Increase your career opportunities: Whether in IT projects, consulting or software development, ISTQB® certification gives you a decisive advantage in the competition for the best jobs.
  • Immediate benefits for companies: Qualified testers are essential for quality assurance. Your company not only saves costs by reducing errors, but also secures the trust of customers.

Course structure in four stages

The course is specially designed to teach you all the important content of software testing step by step. The first three stages help you to consolidate the theoretical knowledge through daily practical exercises. In the fourth stage, you prepare intensively for certification.

  • Practice: Execution of end-to-end test cases for mobile apps and complex web applications

4th stage: Intensive preparation and ISTQB® CTFL certification (days 43-45)

In the final phase, you will prepare intensively for the certification. Our trainers will ensure that you can master the exam confidently and safely by going over all the subject areas again and helping you to internalize what you have learned as best as possible.

Team courses

Practical project management according to SCRUM: your path to successful agile working!

This course offers you a comprehensive and practice-orientated insight into the SCRUM framework, one of the most popular tools in agile project management. In just 5 days, you will learn the most important concepts and techniques to successfully apply SCRUM in your projects. At the end of the course, you will receive a certificate of completion from the WAMOCON Academy, confirming your new skills in agile project management.

Course overview

Day 1: Basics of SCRUM and agility

  • Introduction to the agile mindset and the basic principles of SCRUM
  • Difference between traditional and agile project management
  • The SCRUM process: from the idea to the finished product

Day 2: SCRUM roles and responsibilities

  • The roles in the SCRUM team: product owner, SCRUM master and development team
  • Responsibility and collaboration in the SCRUM process
  • Practical examples and simulations of teamwork

Day 3: SCRUM artefacts and processes

  • Creating and maintaining the product backlog and sprint backlog effectively
  • Sprint planning, implementation and reviews
  • Working with increments and the importance of the ‘Definition of Done’

Day 4: SCRUM events and rituals

  • Daily standups, sprint reviews, sprint retrospectives and sprint planning
  • How to keep meetings short, focussed and productive
  • The value of regular adjustments and optimisations in the team

Day 5: Practical application and SCRUM tools

  • How to use SCRUM tools such as Jira and Trello to organise your team
  • Recognising and overcoming obstacles in the SCRUM process
  • Final project: Implementing your own project using SCRUM principles

What you will take with you after the course

  • Practical knowledge: Not only will you understand how SCRUM works in theory, but you will also be able to apply it to real projects.
  • Work efficiently: Learn how to manage projects in a targeted manner through sprints, clear role allocations and daily meetings.
  • Certificate from the WAMOCON Academy: After successfully completing the course, you will receive a certificate confirming your SCRUM project management skills.

Who is this course suitable for?

  • Project managers who want to switch to agile working methods
  • Team leaders who want to organise their team more efficiently
  • Developers who want to become part of an agile SCRUM team
  • Product owners who want to maximise the value of their products with SCRUM

Aim of the course

After these 5 intensive days, you will be able to apply SCRUM independently in your projects. Whether in software development, marketing or other areas, agile working will make you and your team more efficient and successful.
