Start into a secure future with the best benefits for you

ISTQB® certification is a globally recognized seal of quality for software testers. It opens doors to new career opportunities and helps you to stand your ground in a competitive market. Discover how this certification can positively influence your professional career.

Occupational safety and demand

High demand: Software testers are in high demand in the IT industry, as quality assurance plays a central role in the development of software. Stability: As software development is a continuous process, there is always a need for qualified testers.

Career development and further training

Diverse career paths: Software testers can specialize in different areas, such as automation testing, performance testing or security testing. Further training opportunities: There are numerous certifications and training courses (e.g. ISTQB®) that further improve career opportunities.

Technical skills and knowledge

Technical know-how: Testers work closely with developers and develop a deep understanding of software architectures and programming languages. Problem solving skills: Testers must be analytical and detail-oriented to find and document bugs.

Working environment and flexibility

Teamwork: Software testers often work in agile teams and have the opportunity to work on different projects and with different technologies. Remote work: Many positions in the field of software testing offer the opportunity to work from home.

Personal satisfaction and influence

Quality assurance: Testers play a crucial role in ensuring the quality and reliability of software products, which can lead to a high level of job satisfaction. Impact: Good testers have a direct impact on the end products and can help improve the user experience.

Competitive salary

Software testers generally earn a competitive salary that increases with experience and specialization.

Software tester career and ISTQB® certification: The biggest hurdles for IT newcomers

01

Experience gaps and skills shortages

Experience gaps: Many employers prefer candidates with practical experience, which makes it difficult for newcomers to gain their first professional experience.

High demand for skilled workers: Competition for skilled positions is intense, making it harder for newcomers to gain a foothold.

02

Complexity of ISTQB® certification

Extensive knowledge: ISTQB® certification requires a broad and deep understanding of testing processes and methodologies, which can be challenging for newcomers.

Preparation and exam: Preparing for the exam can be time-consuming and demanding, especially for those who have no previous professional experience.

03

Financial and time resources

Costs for training and exams: ISTQB® certification and related preparation courses can be expensive, which can be a financial burden for beginners.

Time management: Working professionals or students need to manage their time effectively to successfully complete the preparation, which can be an additional challenge.
